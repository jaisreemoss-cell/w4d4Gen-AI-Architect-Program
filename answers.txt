QUERY:
How can I cancel a subscription on the Apple App Store?

ANSWER 1 - ASSISTANT:
To cancel a subscription on the Apple App Store, follow these steps:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your **name** at the top of the screen.
3. Select **Subscriptions**.
4. Find the subscription you want to cancel and tap on it.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the screen.
6. Confirm the cancellation when prompted.

You will receive a confirmation email, and your subscription will remain active until the end of the billing cycle.
